# Say Grace
A Catholic prayer app, dedicated to my late Lola Mercy.
A digital version of the prayer cards she used to say grace before every meal. Since my family is spread out across continents, this app allows everyone to have a copy of the cards to keep Lola in our hearts. 
Uses gesture control to randomise the prayer of the day (akin to shuffling the cards)
Users can add their own custom prayer cards as well :) 
Runs on device to be used on the go/survive Philippines wifi. 


## Usage 
- Can be downloaded and run as-is, either from XCode simulator or downloaded from the App Store here: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/say-grace/id6744123763
- Or the Google Play store, under the same name.
